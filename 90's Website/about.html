About Creed



What is Creed?


Creed is an American post-grunge and alternative rock band formed in Tallahassee,
Florida, in 1994 by singer Scott Stapp, guitarist Mark Tremonti, bassist Brian Marshall, and drummer Scott Phillips.
They became one of the biggest commercial rock acts of the late 1990s and early 2000s, selling over 30 million albums
despite facing heavy criticism from music reviewers.

  • Origins: Formed in Tallahassee, Florida in 1994.
  • Members: Scott Stapp, Mark Tremonti, Brian Marshall, Scott Phillips.
  • Debut: Released My Own Prison in 1997.
  • Success: Sold over 50 million albums worldwide.
  • Grammy: Won Best Rock Song for With Arms Wide Open.
  • Hiatus: Disbanded in 2004; Alter Bridge formed.
  • Reunions: Reunited in 2009 and toured again in 2024.
